Welcome to HandSign!

For the best translation experience, please keep these tips in mind:

  • Allow Camera Access: The translator can't work if it can't see you!
  • Good Lighting: Make sure your hand is well-lit and clear, not in shadow.
  • Clear Background: Avoid cluttered or "busy" backgrounds (like patterns or posters).
  • Full Hand Visible: Ensure your entire hand and wrist are in the camera's view.
  • Camera Range: Keep your hand close and directly in front of the camera for best accuracy.
